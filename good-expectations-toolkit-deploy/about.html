We got tired of the blank prompt box too.

Good Expectations Toolkit exists because owning a powerful AI tool and knowing what to type into it turned out to be two very different skills.

Why we built this

Most people don't have a "prompting" problem — they have a "starting" problem. You open ChatGPT or Claude with a real task in mind, and then spend ten minutes rewriting the same three sentences, unsure whether you're asking for the right thing at all.

We started collecting the prompts, templates and step-by-step workflows that actually got results — for sales outreach, job interviews, content calendars, research, budgeting, and dozens of everyday tasks — and organizing them the way a good reference librarian would: by outcome, not by tool.

That collection became Good Expectations Toolkit: one card catalog you can search by task, copy from, and customize in seconds, no matter which AI platform you're using.

What we believe

A few principles guide what makes it into the library.

Outcomes over novelty

Every prompt earns its place by producing something you can use — a draft, a plan, a decision — not by being clever.

Works everywhere

We test across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Grok, so switching tools doesn't mean starting over.

Editable by design

Placeholders are marked clearly. You should be able to customize a prompt in seconds, not guess where to edit it.

Always growing

The library is updated regularly as new use cases, platforms and techniques prove themselves — at no extra cost to members.

Who's behind it

A small team building in the open

Good Expectations Toolkit is built and maintained by a small team that uses AI tools every day for real work — sales, marketing, hiring, and running a business. Every prompt in the library has been tested against an actual task before it's added, and every category is shaped by what people ask us for most.

Have a use case that isn't covered yet? Tell us what you're working on — a lot of the library started as a request.

Frequently asked

Do I need a specific AI subscription to use the Toolkit?

No. The prompts and workflows are written to work with any major AI platform, including free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, as well as Copilot and Grok.

Is this a subscription?

No. Good Expectations Toolkit is a one-time purchase with lifetime access, including future additions to the library at no extra cost.

How is the library organized?

Into nine categories built around outcomes — business, sales, marketing, career, research, productivity, personal development, technology and everyday life.

How do I access the Toolkit after purchase?

You'll receive access details by email right after checkout. If anything doesn't arrive within a few minutes, contact us and we'll sort it out.
